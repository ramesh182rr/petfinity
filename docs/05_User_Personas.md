# Personas

## Persona A — Busy pet parent
- Needs a convenient appointment and reliable nearby service.
- Pain: calling multiple clinics, unclear prices, appointment uncertainty.
- Success: find a verified provider, see availability and book quickly.

## Persona B — First-time pet parent
- Needs guidance, reminders and confidence.
- Pain: uncertainty about vaccines, care routines, who to trust.
- Success: pet profile, clear care schedule and trusted provider discovery.

## Persona C — Traveling pet parent
- Needs caretaker for one day to several weeks.
- Pain: trust, price, availability, pet-specific instructions.
- Success: compare verified offers, meet provider, book, receive updates.

## Persona D — Veterinarian / clinic owner
- Needs discovery, schedule control and qualified appointments.
- Pain: missed calls, manual scheduling, no-shows and limited digital reach.
- Success: manageable calendar, accurate listing, bookings and patient context.

## Persona E — Independent caretaker
- Needs discoverability and fair work.
- Pain: inconsistent leads, trust barrier, payment disputes and schedule coordination.
- Success: profile, quote requests, bookings, reviews and clear payout rules.

## Persona F — Community-oriented pet parent
- Needs local connections, playdates, advice and events.
- Pain: scattered groups and low-quality/inaccurate information.
- Success: relevant local feed and safe community interactions.

## Persona G — Platform admin/moderator
- Needs provider verification, incident handling and operational visibility.
- Pain: marketplace trust/safety is manual and can become overwhelming.
- Success: audit trail, queues, clear rules, manageable tools and escalation.
