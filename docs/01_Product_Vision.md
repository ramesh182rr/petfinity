# Product Vision — Petfinity (Working Name)

**Status:** Product concept / blueprint v1.0, 23 Nov 2026  
**Working brand:** Petfinity (not trademark/domain/app-store cleared)  
**Initial market:** Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Bengaluru (Karnataka), with focused local clusters and expansion based on supply/demand and service quality.

## Vision
One trusted place for everything a pet needs—and everyone who loves them. Petfinity connects pet parents with local veterinary care, trusted caretakers, useful services and a supportive pet community.

## Mission
Reduce the friction, uncertainty and fragmented coordination involved in caring for pets by making trusted local care easy to discover, compare, book and manage.

## Problem
Pet parents commonly rely on fragmented channels: Google/Maps, phone calls, WhatsApp groups, referrals, social media, clinic records and informal caretaker arrangements. Availability, price, service details, credentials and trust can be hard to compare. Pet-care providers have limited digital discovery and booking tools, and may handle scheduling manually.

## Product thesis
A geographically concentrated, trust-first marketplace can solve a repeated high-value problem (finding and booking care) and use pet profiles, health reminders and community to create retention. Marketplace liquidity—not feature count—is the first success constraint.

## Positioning
**A trusted local pet-care network where pet parents can find verified veterinarians, care providers and nearby pet people—and manage their pet's care in one place.**

## Product pillars
1. **Care:** veterinary discovery, appointment booking, pet health profile and reminders.
2. **Services:** caretakers, boarding, day care, home visits, grooming and future walking/training.
3. **Community:** local discovery, feed, lost/found, events and pet-owner connections.
4. **Shop:** initially affiliate/partner discovery; native commerce only after validation.

## Target outcomes
- Owners can confidently discover and book a relevant service without repeated calls.
- Providers gain qualified local demand and manageable scheduling tools.
- Care requests receive timely, comparable offers.
- Completed transactions create trust signals and repeat usage.

## Non-goals for first release
Nationwide operational coverage; inventory/logistics; custom telemedicine infrastructure; social messaging at scale; AI diagnosis; complex subscriptions; pet insurance underwriting; microservices; live video; a large ecommerce catalog.

## North-star metric
**Monthly completed bookings per active launch-area pet parent**, supported by booking conversion, repeat rate, successful provider fulfillment, cancellation and safety metrics.

## Brand naming exploration
**Selected brand:** Petfinity. Trademark, domain, social-handle and app-store availability checks remain outstanding; no clearance is implied.
